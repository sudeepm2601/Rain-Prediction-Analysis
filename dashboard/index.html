1. Extreme Spatial Disparity (76.4x)
Wettest: Tamenglong (7,229 mm) & Jaintia Hills (6,380 mm).
Driest: Ladakh / Leh (94.6 mm) & Jaisalmer (181 mm).
Regional models require localized baseline scaling.
2. Chronic Drought Belt & Clustering
West Rajasthan suffers drought in 28.7% of years; Saurashtra in 26.1%.
Persistence Trap: If year t-1 was a drought, P(Drought in year t) jumps from 15.1% to 24.8% (+64% risk).
3. June Onset Predictability (61.4%)
When June rainfall suffers a severe deficit (≤ -25%), 61.4% of seasons ended in deficient or below-normal monsoon totals due to truncated moisture absorption.
4. Dual-Monsoon Decoupling
Correlation between SW (Jun-Sep) & NE (Oct-Dec) is r = +0.041 (zero correlation).
Tamil Nadu & Puducherry receive 50-70% of rain in Oct-Dec, decoupled from summer droughts.
